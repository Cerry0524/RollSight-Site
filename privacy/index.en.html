PRIVACY / 2026-09-08

Privacy Policy

Updated 2026-09-08 · Includes the unreleased 2.0 purchase proposal.

1. About RollSight

RollSight is an offline-first attendance and unit administration app provided by an independent developer. It is not affiliated with a government or military authority.

2. Data you create

Units, member names, roles, attendance status, timestamps, notes and seat layouts are primarily stored locally on your device. There is no automatic cloud roster sync or RollSight cloud account. Content may leave your device when you choose to export, share or contact support.

3. QR and file transfer

The new 2.0 sensitive bundle uses per-recipient encryption (X25519, HKDF-SHA256 and XChaCha20-Poly1305) with Ed25519 signatures. Sending and the complete import workflow are not yet released. Transfers are initiated by you through QR codes, images or files. Signatures, device bindings and replay protection reduce source and integrity risks; they do not mean every shared output is encrypted. External messaging or storage services and recipients may retain copies. Check the content and recipient before sharing.

4. Subscriptions and one-time purchase

Version 2.0 and the proposed FULL lifetime purchase are not yet released. The tentative price is NT$99 for a one-time FULL entitlement without an expiry date, usage depletion or automatic renewal, including FULL features and updates within this App with no separate 2.x minor-version fee; the final price, currency and availability are determined by the store payment screen. Version 2.0 offers FREE and a FULL one-time purchase, with no new monthly or annual subscriptions and no automatically charged seven-day trial. Existing BASIC/FULL subscriptions retain their store-confirmed valid period; they are not automatically converted to lifetime. A lifetime purchase will not automatically cancel an existing subscription: manage it in the original store. The store handles payment; the App does not receive credit card details. RevenueCat processes purchase validation and entitlement status, which may involve an anonymous App user identifier and product, transaction and receipt information. Restore purchases with the same account in the original store. Refunds or revoked transactions revoke the associated entitlement. Cross-platform portability and Family Sharing are not guaranteed.

5. Backup and device protection

PIN and supported biometrics protect unlocking; this does not mean the entire local database is encrypted. Exported backups contain device identity and selected tables, encrypted using a PIN-derived key. They are not full database backups. The developer cannot recover your PIN or decrypt your backup. Keep exports and your PIN safe and check the scope before restoring.

6. Diagnostics

If Sentry is enabled in the release, the App may send error stack traces, device, OS and App information for troubleshooting and performance improvement. The code removes selected request data, extras and navigation messages, disables replay and screenshots, and disables default PII collection. Processing depends on the release configuration and provider policies.

7. Control and deletion

You can view and manage local records and clear App data. Expiry or downgrade does not itself delete local data but re-applies feature limits. Uninstalling, clearing data or losing a device may make local data unrecoverable. Your exports and recipients’ copies are not deleted with the App. Restoring a purchase does not restore rosters.

8. Third-party services

Apple App Store handles purchases; RevenueCat manages entitlements; Sentry processes diagnostics when enabled. See the Apple Privacy Policy, RevenueCat Privacy Policy and Sentry Privacy Policy. This website is served by GitHub Pages; visits are also subject to that service’s data processing.

9. Contact and changes

Contact cerry0524@gmail.com for privacy questions. Your message is used to handle your request; do not send PINs, keys or unredacted rosters. Material processing changes will be reflected here. The policy must be checked against the actual release before version 2.0 is published.
